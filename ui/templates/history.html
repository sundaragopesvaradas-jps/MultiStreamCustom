How to read this

Delivered
Video was accepted by the destination for the whole session.
Connected, no video sent
The push started but no data moved — usually a dead or expired stream key.
Failed to connect
The destination refused the key or the endpoint was unreachable.

If a destination says Delivered but nothing appeared on the page, the stream reached the platform and the platform did not publish it. On Facebook that almost always means the “Go Live” button in Live Producer was never pressed.
